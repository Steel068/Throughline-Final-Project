Counsel for Rosa Alvarez submits this demand for settlement of all claims arising from the collision of February 14, 2026.

LIABILITY

On February 14, 2026, at approximately 5:40 p.m., Ms. Alvarez was stopped at the signal at Marina Parkway and Ninth Street.
Your insured, Daniel Prewitt, struck her vehicle from behind without braking.
The investigating officer cited Mr. Prewitt for following too closely.
The investigating officer observed no skid marks before the point of impact.
Your insured stated to the officer that he had looked away from the roadway.
The collision was entirely avoidable and resulted from your insured's inattention.
Liability is not reasonably in dispute.

INJURIES AND TREATMENT

Ms. Alvarez was transported by ambulance to Beacon Regional Hospital the evening of the collision.
Emergency department staff documented cervical strain and lumbar strain.
Examination that evening documented tenderness of the posterior cervical musculature.
Ms. Alvarez was discharged in stable condition at 9:56 p.m.
Radiographs taken that evening showed no acute fracture.
She was discharged the same evening with instructions to follow up.
Ms. Alvarez first consulted Dr. Ellen Whitaker, orthopedic surgeon, on February 19, 2026.
Dr. Whitaker documented reduced cervical range of motion and tenderness at C5 through C7.
Dr. Whitaker recorded upper extremity strength as full and symmetric.
An MRI of the cervical spine was performed on February 26, 2026.
The MRI demonstrated a disc protrusion at C5-C6 with mild foraminal narrowing.
The protrusion measured approximately three millimetres.
Ms. Alvarez began physical therapy at Harbor Physical Therapy on February 24, 2026.
She completed eleven physical therapy sessions between February 24 and April 30, 2026.
Her therapist documented persistent limitation in cervical rotation at discharge.
Cervical rotation measured sixty degrees to the right at the ninth session.
Ms. Alvarez continued to report headaches through May 2026.
She has been unable to lift her daughter since the collision.
The injuries described above have fundamentally altered her daily life.

SPECIAL DAMAGES

Beacon Regional Hospital emergency services total $3,180.00.
Dr. Ellen Whitaker, orthopedic evaluation, totals $1,450.00.
Westbrook Imaging Center, cervical MRI, totals $2,100.00.
Harbor Physical Therapy, eleven sessions, totals $4,820.00.
Total medical specials are $11,950.00.

WAGE LOSS

Ms. Alvarez is employed by Calloway Logistics as a dispatch coordinator.
She was absent from work from February 15 through March 6, 2026.
Her employer confirms lost earnings of $2,840.00 for that period.

DEMAND

The documented economic losses total $14,790.00.
Given the duration of treatment, the objective MRI findings, and the ongoing limitation documented at discharge, a settlement in the amount of $72,000.00 is appropriate.
